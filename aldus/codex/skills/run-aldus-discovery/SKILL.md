# run-aldus-discovery

This is one of Aldus's procedures. The procedure itself is served by the Aldus connector, not kept in this plugin.

1. Before any step it covers, call the Aldus `get_procedure` tool with the name `run-aldus-discovery` and read it in full. When this session exposes native image generation and the author has not chosen Aldus's image service, include `native_image_generation: true`. Follow it through the Aldus tools, as its first lines say.
2. Read it again whenever your context was compacted or summarized, before you go on with its steps. Never work from memory of it.
3. It applies to work on an Aldus book, inside a job you took with `take_job`, and to research outside a book: ideas you find, or the author's own idea you assess, kept in Research with `keep_ideas`. The `$aldus` skill says how that work runs.
